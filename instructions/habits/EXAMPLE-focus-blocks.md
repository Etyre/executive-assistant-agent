# Example: deep-focus blocks (replace or delete)

- **What**: at least one fully absorbed focus span of 90+ minutes on a weekday.
- **Why**: the research and writing work that matters only moves in long uninterrupted stretches.
- **Rule / TAP**: when the morning checklist is done → start the top task's Toggl timer and the Focus
  Monitor before opening any inbox.
- **Metric**: Focus Monitor `longest_focus_min` ≥ 90 on weekdays. Off track = two weekdays in a row
  under 60, or a 7-day mean under 75.
- **Status**: settling since 2026-10.
- **Known blockers**: meetings before 11:00 fragment the morning; Slack open on the second screen.
